# Design Direction

## Concept: Street Cart Editorial
Create a digital interpretation of hand-painted street-food signage + modern food editorial design. Use oversized typography, irregular but intentional compositions, cut-out food photography, sticker-like labels, ingredient callouts, paper/menu-board textures used sparingly, and energetic micro-motion.

## Visual cues from supplied material
- Fruit green as a core identity cue.
- Citrus/orange/yellow for appetite and emphasis.
- Deep charcoal/near-black inspired by the Chips Adda board.
- White/cream for breathing space.
- Red/pink accents may be used sparingly for spice/fruit cues.

## Avoid the AI-template look
DO NOT default to: generic gradient hero; centered headline + two buttons + floating mockup; glassmorphism; endless rounded cards; 3-column feature grids; random blobs; fake metrics; fake testimonials; stock restaurant imagery; purple/blue SaaS palettes; identical section geometry; gratuitous parallax; huge border radii everywhere.

## Desired feel
Designed, not generated. Some asymmetry is encouraged. Each major section needs its own composition while sharing one visual grammar. The page should still be easy to scan and accessible.
