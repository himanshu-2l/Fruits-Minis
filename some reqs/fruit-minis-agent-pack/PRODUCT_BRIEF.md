# Product Brief

## Objective
Turn social-media discovery into an appetizing digital storefront that makes a visitor understand the concept in seconds and motivates an in-person visit or contact once verified contact/location details are supplied.

## Audience
Students, young adults, families and casual street-food customers. Mobile-first. Visitors may arrive from Instagram/Reels and have low patience.

## Brand personality
Fresh, playful, energetic, affordable, street-food-first, colorful and tactile. It should feel local and human rather than corporate.

## Core story
Two complementary cravings: fresh fruit chaat and crunchy customizable Chips Adda. The experience should celebrate mixing, crunch, freshness and customization.

## Success criteria
- Concept understandable above the fold.
- Chips Adda pricing is immediately scannable.
- Fruit chaat is treated as a primary offering, not an afterthought.
- Strong mobile experience and fast load.
- Easy social/contact/location actions when details become available.
- Visually memorable without sacrificing usability.
