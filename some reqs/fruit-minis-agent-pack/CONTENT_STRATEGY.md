# Content Strategy

Copy should be short, appetizing and conversational. Avoid corporate restaurant language such as "culinary excellence" or "gastronomic journey."

Possible headline territory (creative copy, not factual claims):
- Fresh meets crunch.
- Pick a bag. We make it chatpata.
- Fruit. Crunch. Chaat. Repeat.

Verified brand tagline can be used exactly: "Small Bites. Big Freshness."

For Chips Adda, clearly explain that pricing depends on the base chip-bag tier shown on the supplied menu. Avoid suggesting listed packaged-food brands sponsor Fruit Minis.

For missing data, maintain a centralized content object with null values and conditionally hide sections/controls. Never ship `TBD`, lorem ipsum, fabricated reviews, fake opening hours or fake addresses to production.
