FIRST: read every `.md` file in this repository's handoff pack and inspect all supplied reference images. Do not write implementation code until you can summarize the verified business facts, unknown facts, design constraints and proposed page composition.

1. Never fabricate business facts. Missing location, hours, phone, reviews, fruit-chaat menu/prices and YouTube details remain absent until supplied.
2. Fruit Minis serves fruit chaat and has a Chips Adda concept. Both must feel central.
3. Preserve verified Chips Adda tier information exactly from `BUSINESS_CONTEXT.md`.
4. Avoid the recognizable AI-template aesthetic: no generic gradient hero, floating dashboard mockups, glass cards, repetitive 3-card grids, random blobs, fake stats/testimonials, generic stock photos, or a page made entirely of rounded rectangles.
5. Design mobile-first and make it feel like a digital extension of a lively street-food cart: bold signage, fruit freshness, crunchy texture, tactile menu language, editorial asymmetry, purposeful motion.
6. Every section must earn its place. Do not add filler.
7. Accessibility, performance and responsive behavior are product requirements, not cleanup tasks.

## Workflow
A. Audit the handoff and assets.
B. Propose one coherent art direction and page composition in text.
C. Define tokens and content data.
D. Build structural sections with semantic HTML.
E. Add visual styling and responsive composition.
F. Add restrained purposeful motion.
G. Test all breakpoints, keyboard, reduced motion, typecheck/lint/build.
H. Perform a ruthless visual critique. Identify anything that looks templated, generic, repetitive, poorly spaced or fake, then fix it.

Do not stop at "functional." The finish line is a distinctive, polished, production-quality small-business site.
